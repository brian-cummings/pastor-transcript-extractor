# Proposed projection estimands V1

These targets are proposals for later implementation/validation. No pastor estimator or rate is executed here. E0 is the only active descriptive scope of the prepared displays: supplied local examples. All numeric projection, recurrence and comparison outputs in the packet are null. No counts, probabilities or thresholds are added to the measurement questions.

## Population and missingness

Define the target corpus before deciding which units are measurable: named pastor identity/membership policy, eligible content, source/transcript versions, sermon dates, series, selection mechanism and compatible measurement semantics. Report all eligible, measured, excluded, incompatible and unresolved counts separately. A measured-sample summary describes that sample, not an unobserved pastor's entire ministry.

For E1, proposed `Y_s` is 1 if a supported occurrence exists and 0 only after a construct-complete negative assessment; otherwise it is unknown. The fully assessed sample mean is `sum(Y_s) / number assessed`. Disclose eligible-but-unassessed sermons alongside it, and do not call the complete-case fraction an estimate of the full eligible corpus without a separately justified sampling/missingness treatment. Where a fixed complete eligible corpus is actually known, transparent missing-outcome bounds may later be proposed; none are computed here. Sampling error, measurement error and coverage missingness remain separate uncertainties. Model answer probabilities are not population confidence intervals.

For E2, retain actual block duration and original score distributions. Do not let the number of candidates per sermon weight a pastor's attention pattern. A sermon lacking compatible topic measurement is unknown, not zero. The existing registry's measured-eligible-time scope is retained; a future complete-sermon target would require coverage evidence beyond that proposal.

For E3, define the opportunity **independently of the act/facet whose frequency is estimated**. A text-explanation opportunity, response-development opportunity and human-address opportunity are different populations. Keep opportunities where the act is absent, unresolved or incompletely bounded. A rate over fully assessed opportunities must expose the rest. Episode-weighted occurrence answers “what fraction of opportunities?”; an equal-sermon mean of within-sermon rates answers “what does the typical measured sermon do when opportunities occur?” Report these separately, and leave sermons with unknown or zero relevant opportunities outside that conditional mean with an explicit count. Neither is a whole-sermon occurrence rate.

## Deduplication and dependence

Before any future count, reconcile duplicate recordings and overlapping candidates, determine reviewed episode boundaries, and retain evidence aliases. Multiple facets can share one utterance; they are not independent corroborations. Different actual sermons sharing an illustration count as separate sermon occurrences but remain correlated through material and series. Report series/period distributions and sensitivity; do not invent an independence correction or universal effective N.

Proposed arithmetic contrast, **not measured data**: four candidate locators covering one passage produce one local occurrence; two actual sermons reusing that passage produce two sermon occurrences with shared material dependence. None of those counts supplies the number of assessed sermons or opportunities. Even two act-positive examples cannot justify “2/2 sermons” or “100% of grief opportunities.”

## Gates for stronger statements

Activation requires a separate implementation decision, reviewed semantic compatibility and ownership, candidate-discovery/assessment coverage, production calibration, actual denominator definitions/measurements, feature-specific sufficiency and uncertainty policy, series/time sensitivity and request economics. Existing allowed-use gates remain binding. No universal numerical recurrence cutoff is proposed, and no local example is promoted merely because a minimum count is reached.

Comparison would additionally require a declared comparable cohort and compatible subject/circumstance, recipient type, corpus exposure, semantics and uncertainty. It is held, as are pastor projection, G4 and quality scoring. Missingness must never become a distance/cluster feature or a proxy for being less caring, less distinctive or lower quality.
