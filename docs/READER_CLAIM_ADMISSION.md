## Method correction (implementation revision 1)

The B1 and worldly-yielding diagnostics demonstrate disagreement with the intended
generic candidate-content predicate, without explaining its cause. This method
does not claim to repair that unknown cause. It replaces the admission design:
judge the relation between the reader claim's concrete human action/attitude, its
actor and speech role, and the complete source. Do not ask again whether a string
contains a generic response. No model or threshold change is authorized.

| Existing protection | New method |
| --- | --- |
| Literal source and coordinate checks, immutable producer receipts | Reconstruct the original request and observation; retain these checks. |
| Self-contained response correctness, including scope, polarity, speech status and meaning-changing qualifications | Reuse the exact compatible saved correctness answer at .90. Do not repurchase uncertain answers. Edited evaluation controls require their own correctness answer under the same question. |
| Candidate specificity/presence, preventing background/absence/metacommentary from counting as a finding | Replace with source-grounded human action: the actual claim must name a concrete human action or responsive attitude, with its actor and requested/reported/performed/hypothetical role supported at this source point. Divine action alone cannot pass. New contract, request and receipt; .90 gate. |
| Overlap between source correctness and new action grounding | Intentional: correctness protects the whole description; action grounding tests whether the proposed human act and actor/role are actually evidenced. Neither answer supplies the other. No averaging. |
| Ordinary whole-statement analysis support and dependency-safe document release | Retain unchanged after upstream qualification; no late extraction or authored replacement prose. |
| Complete binding, production admission and quotation holds | Remain held. A dimension-only result grants none of these authorities. |

Runtime descriptions are unchanged machine-generated observations. Human-authored
controls are evaluation-only and must never enter assembly. Rejected old presence
results are historical results under their original contract, never relabeled.
